## Overview

Sparked Host runs game servers for Minecraft and dozens of other games, alongside VPS and dedicated servers.

I redesigned the website page by page: the game landing pages, the game catalogue, free trials, VPS hosting, dedicated servers, the control panel, and company pages such as About Us, Partnerships and a new careers site.

## The brief

![A selection of the page types in the redesign, from game pages to careers](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/scope.webp)

The brief listed every page with the sections it needed, plus notes on what would make each one stand out. A few of those shaped the whole project:

- **Show the games and plans straight away.** The landing page hero needed popular games and their packages, not just a headline.
- **A panel section that isn't a copy of a competitor's.** Shockbyte's was named specifically.
- **Three ways to sell plans.** Two versions built on packages and one with a slider, each letting customers pick a location and hardware line alongside the plan, with free trials mentioned near the top.
- **Dedicated servers that feel different.** A design distinct from the game pages, with location picking and filters.
- **Product pages that know when stock runs out.** Out-of-stock servers switch their call to action from "buy" to "join the waitlist".

## Process

![How the hero evolved across six versions, ending with the game shelf](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/process.webp)

The hero went through more versions than anything else. It started as a left-aligned hero with plan tiers underneath, then a version where hovering a plan dims the background and brings that plan into focus.

From there it split into two directions: a "game shelf" with Sparked's other games stacked down the right, and a centred layout that leaned on plan details, including a highlighted best-seller deal. The final version went back to the game shelf. It does what the brief asked, showing the range of games before anyone scrolls, while the popular plans sit right under the headline.

## Three ways to pick a plan

![The four-step plan picker, with every step open on the left and steps unlocking one at a time on the right](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/plans-steps.webp)

The first two versions share the same four numbered steps: server type, location, plan, then review and purchase. In one, every step is open at once, so experienced customers can go straight to what they want. In the other, each step unlocks only after the one before it, with a short message saying what to pick first, so newer customers never get lost.

Each location shows a small signal icon for estimated connection quality. Anyone unsure which server type to choose can compare them or contact sales without leaving the flow.

## Or browse them all

![Plans grouped by server type, each with its own carousel](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/plans-carousel.webp)

The third version skips the steps. Each server type (Vanilla, Modded and Community) gets its own row, with what's included in every plan listed at the top and a carousel of plans underneath, named after Minecraft blocks such as Copper, Emerald and Netherite. It suits people who would rather compare prices at a glance.

## A panel section of its own

![The panel section, listing the Apollo panel's tools next to an illustration of the panel](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/panel.webp)

Rather than another static screenshot of a control panel, this section lists the Apollo panel's tools down the left side (console, file manager, modpack installer, plugin installer and more) next to a simplified illustration of the panel itself. It explains what customers can do without asking them to read a wall of features.

## Dedicated servers

![The dedicated server list with location, quick filters and sortable columns](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/dedicated.webp)

Dedicated servers needed their own look, so the page leads with a filterable list instead of plan cards. Customers pick a country, apply quick filters such as AMD, Intel, big storage or bandwidth, and sort by CPU, memory, storage, setup time or price. Each row shows what matters for a bare metal server at a glance, with a deploy button at the end.

## When stock runs out

![The same dedicated server product page in stock with Add to Cart, and out of stock with Join The Waitlist](../../../assets/work/sparked-host/stock.webp)

Every dedicated server gets a product page with full specifications for the processor, memory, storage and bandwidth. Stock updates automatically, so I designed both states. In stock, the page shows the location and an "Add to Cart" button. Out of stock, the same spot turns red and the button becomes "Join The Waitlist", so interested customers aren't lost.

## Outcome

Beyond the pages shown here, the project also covered free trials, VPS hosting, the Apollo panel page, About Us, Partnerships, an affiliate program, DDoS protection, a Google Ads landing page and a careers site with job listings and applications.

Sparked Host's current site is at [sparkedhost.com](https://sparkedhost.com).
