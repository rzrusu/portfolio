Modern Hosting had one problem a lot of multi-game hosts share: the homepage has to speak to someone who plays Enshrouded and someone who plays Minecraft at the same time, and a generic "game hosting" headline speaks to neither.

The answer is a hero that changes with the game. A row of game icons sits at the bottom right with arrows on either side. Pick one, and the headline, the starting price, the recommended player count and the background art all switch to that game. The first thing a visitor reads is the name of the game they came for, and the price they would actually pay for it, without a carousel rotating on its own.

The rest of the homepage is deliberately quieter. Six features in a grid, the locations as a dotted world map with nine pins and nothing else, and a DDoS section that leads with the numbers (attacks mitigated, attack types covered, protection capacity) in cards rather than paragraphs. Headings throughout use a wide uppercase face, which gives the site its character without the hero having to carry all of it.

The site is live at modernhosting.com.
