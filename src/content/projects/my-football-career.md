## About the Project

My Football Career is a mobile football career simulation game currently in development. The game allows players to experience the life of a professional football player, making decisions that affect their career trajectory.

## Features

- **Career Mode**: Start from youth academy and work your way up to the top leagues
- **Dynamic Events**: Random events that affect your player's career
- **Skill Development**: Train and improve your player's abilities
- **Transfer Market**: Negotiate contracts and move between clubs

## Technology Stack

- Built with Flutter for cross-platform support
- Firebase for backend services
- Custom game engine for simulation logic
