## Project Overview

Sparked Host is a game server hosting company that needed a modern, professional website to showcase their services and attract new customers.

## My Role

I was responsible for the complete website redesign, focusing on:

- **Visual Design**: Creating a modern, clean aesthetic that appeals to gamers
- **User Experience**: Streamlined navigation and clear service presentation
- **Responsive Design**: Ensuring the site works perfectly on all devices

## Design Approach

The design emphasizes trust and reliability - key factors for customers choosing a hosting provider. I used a dark theme with accent colors to appeal to the gaming audience while maintaining professionalism.

## Results

The new design helped increase conversion rates and establish Sparked Host as a premium option in the game hosting market.
